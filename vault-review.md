## C. Citation format (the biggest source of inconsistency)

Right now there are at least five styles for the same kind of source:
- `^[צא:ה]` · `^[שו"ע צא:ה]` · `^[שו"ע יו"ד צא:ה]` · `ס' צב סע' ד` · `^[פז:ג]` (with no author)
- ש"ך: `ש"ך ס' צא ס"ק יח` · `ש"ך יו"ד ס' צא ס"ק ז` · `שם ס"ק ח` · `ס' צב ס"ק יא`
- Some citations are in the body in parentheses (מדות L8 `(see פ"ת יו"ד ס' צא ס"ק א)`, בישול L23 `(א"ח ס' שיח)`, בב"ח L13 `(:ת"ה הקצר …)`) and others are footnotes.

**Proposed standard:**
1. **Default חלק is יורה דעה, in every note.** בשר בחלב already says so at the top (L5). Put the same one-line note on every Halacha note, or once on a section page, and **drop `יו"ד`** from the citations (תערובות has 7, בישול 2, plus the body-text ones). Write `או"ח`/`חו"מ`/`אה"ע` only when it isn't יו"ד.
2. **שו"ע / רמ"א:** `שו"ע צא:ה` and `רמ"א צא:ה` (siman:se'if). Always name the author: no bare `^[פז:ג]` and no `ס' X סע' Y`.
3. **Commentaries on the סעיף:** `ש"ך צא ס"ק יח`, `ט"ז צא ס"ק ז`, `פ"ת צא ס"ק ח`. Drop `ס'` for consistency with #2.
4. **`שם`** is fine immediately after a full citation of the same source, but not across headings. The 26 bare `^[שם]` in בב"ח are hard to resolve when a section is embedded elsewhere (embeds lose the preceding citation).
5. **Sources go in footnotes `^[…]`, never in body parentheses.** The one `[^1]` reference footnote (בישול L12/L14) should become inline `^[…]` like the other ~325.
6. **Gemara links:** currently mixed `[[:גמ' חולין קטו](hebrewbooks)`, `[[:חולין קיג](…)`, `[רשי חולין קיג: …]`, `[חולין ק"ה](…)`, plus one Sefaria link. Proposed: `[חולין קטו:](link)`, with the amud marker after the daf, no `גמ'`, and the daf with no gershayim. Pick one host. **Sefaria** deep-links to the amud; the hebrewbooks links can't (L1 שולחן `daf=105` vs `daf=115b`).
